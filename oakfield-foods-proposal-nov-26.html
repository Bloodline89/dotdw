01 · New markets · how it works

Three specialist AI agents, working like a translation agency.

1

Research

A terminology agent builds a glossary of trade terms for each country, checked against live local sources.

2

Translate

A translation agent works page by page from that glossary, keeping every heading, list and table in place.

3

Review

A senior review agent checks every paragraph against the English. Nothing added, dropped or invented.

4

Publish for search

Page addresses, metadata and country labels are set for Google. Your team sense-checks the wording.

03 Content and site care · 16

03 · Site care · schema

Schema is a set of hidden labels that tells Google and AI the facts, so they do not have to guess.

Visitors never see it. Today no page on the site has any.

LabelWhere it goesWhat it does for you
OrganizationHome and AboutYour name, address, founding year and certifications. Feeds Google's company panel.
ServiceThe four service pagesWhat each service is and where you offer it.
Article and authorEvery weekly articleWho wrote it and when, so AI can credit Oakfield.
BreadcrumbAll pagesA cleaner address line under your link on Google.

Follows schema.org and the structured data guidelines Google publishes. Included in the £800 a month.
