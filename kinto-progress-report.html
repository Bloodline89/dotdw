Executive Summary

The technical foundations from the audit are live, and Google's response is measurable

Between April and June the development team delivered four of the audit's structural recommendations on the EU store: canonical product links, the removal of 9,539 redirect chains, an automated hreflang sitemap, and self-referencing canonicals on product variants with matching schema. The clearest result is that Google can now see far more of the catalogue. Search terms reaching product variant pages have grown six-fold, over 7,000 new keywords have appeared since the changes went live, non-brand impressions moved ahead of last year in August for the first time in 2026, and August conversion from organic traffic is 26% higher than last August.

The Programme

Four structural changes, delivered in three months

Every change below came directly from the audit's priority list. Because they are structural rather than cosmetic, their effects compound: cleaner crawling feeds better indexing, which feeds more rankings.

7 April

Redirect chains eliminated

The search modal colour swatches were rebuilt to link directly to products, removing all 9,539 redirect chains on the EU store and freeing crawl budget for real pages.

7 & 23 April

Canonical links and hreflang

Collection pages now pass link equity to canonical product URLs, and an automated hreflang sitemap connects the EU store's four language versions and the UK store.

28 May

Variants made indexable

Colour and size variants received self-referencing canonicals, giving Google a dedicated page for every variant for the first time.

11 June

Variant-aware schema

Product schema and social metadata were updated so each variant page describes itself accurately to Google's shopping and AI systems.

Result 02  ·  Non-Brand Reach

Kinto is being found by people who were not searching for Kinto

Brand demand is stable, which is expected for a loved brand. The growth is all in non-brand discovery: impressions for generic product searches rose from 146,000 in January to 207,000 in July, and non-brand searches now account for 70% of query-reported impressions, up from 57% in January. The seasonality-adjusted view is even clearer: against the same months last year, non-brand impressions went from 24% behind in May to level in July and 13% ahead in the first three weeks of August, the first year on year growth in 2026. This is the store starting to compete for new customers, not just serving existing ones.

Why impressions lead clicks. Impressions are the leading indicator: Google shows a page, measures engagement, then promotes it. The 7,373 new keywords currently average position 8 or below. The next phase of the roadmap, collection page headings and descriptions, exists precisely to convert this new visibility into top-3 positions and clicks. The 135 EU-market keywords the audit identified within striking distance of the top 3 are still there waiting for that on-page layer.
Result 03  ·  Richer Listings

Kinto listings now show prices where they used to show plain links

The audit showed competitors winning attention with rich results while Kinto appeared as a plain blue link. Since the June schema update, that has begun to reverse, and Merchant Centre confirms it from Google's own shopping data. Free product listings had been in steep decline all year, down 95% year on year in January before the programme began. Since April they have recovered month after month, a climb with no seasonal precedent: over the same April to July stretch last year, free listing impressions were flat. August is the first month of year on year growth. The feed now carries 751 variant-level listings with colour and size in the title, in local languages, and those listings earned every free-listing click this year.

Result 04  ·  AI Visibility

AI assistants are sending Kinto more shoppers every month

Search is expanding beyond the ten blue links. AI Overviews now appear on Kinto's category terms such as "best travel tumbler" and "best reusable water bottle", and AI assistants are becoming a real acquisition channel. The structured data shipped in June is exactly what these systems read: sessions referred by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot grew 66% between January and July, and August is tracking to be the strongest month this year.

Result 05  ·  Traffic Quality

Every organic session is worth more than it was in January

Organic traffic follows a strong seasonal curve for a gifting-led brand, peaking in November and December, so within-year movements need care. Two views tell the same story. Within 2026, conversion and revenue per session have climbed every quarter. And in the one month where a clean year on year comparison exists, August, conversion from organic traffic is 26% higher than last August (2.82% vs 2.24%) and revenue per session is 13% higher (€2.05 vs €1.82). Visitors arriving through search are increasingly landing on the exact product, colour and size they wanted, and they buy more often.

Result 06  ·  International Foundation

The two stores are learning to stay out of each other's way

Before April, the EU and UK stores were invisible to each other in search and competed head-on in UK results. The automated hreflang sitemap went live on 23 April, correctly formatted and fully reciprocal, and Google is beginning to consolidate: the EU store's generic rankings in UK results have reduced sharply since spring, while the UK store's impressions have doubled since January. That is the intended shape, with each store serving its own market.

The Next Phase

The foundations are earning; the next layer converts visibility into clicks

Everything delivered so far was infrastructure. The visibility it created is the raw material for the next phase, which is where the click and revenue growth compounds.

1

Collection page on-page layer

Headings, titles and descriptions for the priority collections in all four languages. This is the unlock for the 135 keywords already within striking distance of the top 3.

2

Homepage into the hreflang sitemap

Completes brand-search consolidation between the EU and UK stores in every market.

3

Review schema

Adds star ratings to Kinto's own listings, matching the treatment stockists and marketplaces already receive.

4

Localised category content

Native-language landing content for high-volume category terms in Germany, France and the Netherlands, such as trinkflasche, gourde isotherme and thermosfles.
