Monthly Click Trend Context — GSC
Monthly clicks in 2026 to date: Jan 8,645 · Feb 11,543 · Mar 13,500 · Apr 11,177 · May 4,511 (14 days, pace ~9,667).

Why April looks lower than March: March had a peak week (23-29 March = 4,234 clicks) which sits well above the usual weekly run rate of ~2,500–3,000. April returned to a normal cadence (avg ~2,570 clicks/week). This was a one-off March uplift, not an April decline.

In 2025, April was naturally higher than March (April 10,649 vs March 8,739 = +21.9%). In 2026, this seasonal pattern reversed because of the March peak. Year-on-year, every month of 2026 is ahead of 2025.

Google AI Mode Brand Visibility (BOFU Test) — 252 prompts
Separately, we ran 252 bottom-of-funnel comparison prompts through Google's AI Mode (e.g. 'best UK brand for tulle skirts under 150 pounds') to test brand visibility for high-intent buying queries. Eleven Loves was cited in 56 of 252 prompts = 22.2% visibility — and every single mention is at position 1 (the top citation). Strongest categories: Tulle Styling (90%), Body Confidence (67%), Tulle Skirts (60%). The brand is being cited with specific products (Iris Tulle Maxi, Billie Barrel Leg Trousers, Poppy Puff Sleeve Blouse) and links to blog content (style guides, how-to articles), confirming the SEO investment in tulle and styling content is paying off in AI search.
Source: DataForSEO Google AI Mode API run on a custom 252-prompt BOFU question set. Note: AI Mode responses are dynamic and vary by query, location and crawl — the same prompt run on different days can produce different brand sets. Track the trend rather than the single-run percentage.

Top competitors in AI Mode: ME+EM (170), Marks & Spencer (120), Boden (67), Whistles (58), Reiss (46), Jigsaw (44), Hush (37), COS (33), Phase Eight (30), Hobbs (26). Eleven Loves is now competing in the same AI-recommended set as Reiss, Whistles, Boden, ME+EM and M&S specifically within tulle and French style queries.

LLM Brand Visibility (training-data LLMs)
Across 799 queries to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini: 23 mentions (2.9% visibility). Perplexity is the strongest LLM finding the brand (9.1%) because it uses live web search. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini draw from training data and don't yet reference Eleven Loves — this requires building third-party authority (press, fashion blogs, listicles) to enter their knowledge base.
